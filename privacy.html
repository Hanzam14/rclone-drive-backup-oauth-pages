Privacy policy

rclone Drive Backup is a personal desktop setup used by its owner to upload and verify backups in the owner's Google Drive.

It requests the drive.file Google OAuth scope. rclone stores its OAuth credentials on the owner's Windows computer and transfers backup files between that computer and the owner's Google Drive. This informational website does not receive backup files or OAuth tokens.

The setup does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings.
